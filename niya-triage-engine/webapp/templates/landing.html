Tell us what is going on, in your own words.

We read what you write, work out what kind of support fits, and show you the counsellors best placed to help - usually in under a minute.

How it works

  1. Write freely. No forms full of dropdowns, no diagnosis to pick from.
  2. We match you to counsellors by what you need, the language you want to speak, and who is actually free when you are.
  3. Book a time in your own timezone and pay. You will get a reminder before it starts.

If you are in immediate danger, this is not the right place. Please contact your local emergency number or a crisis line now. This service is not monitored around the clock.

What we do with what you write

Your words are used to route you to the right person and are then discarded. We keep only a version with phone numbers, email addresses and locations removed, so a coordinator reviewing an urgent case has context. We never sell data, and we do not share it with your university or employer.
